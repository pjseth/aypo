# Draft FOI request — FDA Philippines food product registrations

**Submit via:** eFOI portal, https://www.foi.gov.ph → Department of Health → Food and Drug Administration.
The portal asks for an account and a valid government ID. Fill in the bracketed fields before sending.

**Precedent to cite:** an earlier eFOI request titled "FDA Database" asked for the FDA's registered
product database in Excel/CSV and is shown as granted: https://www.foi.gov.ph/agencies/doh/fda-database/

---

**Title:** Complete list of food product registrations (CPR), including expired and cancelled, in CSV or Excel

**Purpose:** Research — analysis of product registration trends in the Philippine processed-food sector.

**Request:**

Good day. I respectfully request a copy of the FDA's food product registration records (Certificates of
Product Registration for processed food products and raw materials), in CSV or Excel format, covering:

1. All registrations currently valid, **and** all registrations that have expired, lapsed or been cancelled,
   from the earliest year available (or at least from 1 January 2015) to the date of release.
2. Registrations issued under both the earlier e-registration system (FR-4000… numbers) and the eServices
   system introduced under FDA Circular No. 2026-0002 (FR-1… / FR-2… numbers).

For each registration, the following fields, where available:

- Registration (CPR) number
- Product name and brand name
- Food product classification (raw material; low, medium or high risk)
- Registered company (license holder) and its License to Operate number
- Manufacturer name and country of manufacture
- Packaging material
- Application type (initial, renewal, amendment, re-application) and decision date
- Validity / expiry date
- Status (valid, expired, cancelled, suspended) and date of status change

I understand some fields may be withheld under the FOI exceptions; I would be grateful for the rest of the
record set. A similar request ("FDA Database") was previously granted through this portal.

Thank you.
